## Overview
**Avatar: The Last Airbender** is an animated fantasy series about Aang, the last surviving Airbender, who must master all four elements to restore balance to the world. It has *action*, *humor*, and ~~boring filler~~ lots of heart.

## Why I Love It
- Great character development
  - Zuko's redemption arc is one of the best in animation
  - Every member of the team grows across the seasons
- Amazing world-building
  - Four distinct nations
  - Detailed cultures and bending styles
- Memorable soundtrack and fight scenes

> My take: this show proves that a "kids' series" can have deep themes about war, family, and growing up.

## Characters
1. **Aang** - the Avatar and last Airbender
2. **Katara** - waterbender and the heart of the group
3. **Sokka** - the strategist and comic relief
4. **Toph** - the greatest earthbender
5. **Zuko** - the exiled prince turned firebending teacher
